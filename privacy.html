Privacy Policy — PharmaReg Hub

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the PharmaReg Hub Android app and the web version at liangxi2025-code.github.io/pharmareg-hub

Summary

PharmaReg Hub is a reference tool for drug-regulatory information. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Your preferences (language, light/dark theme) and recently downloaded reference data are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • If you choose to enter your own AI (LLM) API key, it is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

Network requests the app makes

  • Reference-data updates — the app downloads the latest public datasets (regulations, guidance lists, CDE registration records) from GitHub Pages and the jsDelivr CDN. These requests contain no personal information.
  • FDA label search — when you search a drug name, the search term is sent to the U.S. FDA's public openFDA API (api.fda.gov).
  • AI features (optional) — only if you configure your own API key, the text you submit to an AI feature is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you configured (for example Zhipu AI). That provider's own privacy policy applies.
  • Official websites — links to regulators' websites open in your browser and are subject to those sites' policies.

Children

The app is intended for pharmaceutical and regulatory professionals and is not directed at children.

Disclaimer

Content is provided for reference only. Always rely on the official text published by the relevant authority.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/liangxi2025-code/pharmareg-hub/issues.
